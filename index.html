Conference Preparation: Process Guide

This document outlines the end-to-end workflow for Academy presenters delivering conference sessions, ensuring consistency from the initial stakeholder request through the final debrief. The process is organized into four main phases: Event Intake & Logistics, Session Preparation, Engagement Setup, and Post-Conference Wrap-Up.

Last updated: September 26th, 2026

Phase 1: Event Intake & Logistics

This phase ensures the event is properly vetted and that you have all necessary stakeholder alignment and travel arrangements secured.

1. Official Confirmation & Assignment

  • Request Vetting: Rachel acts as the primary point of contact for requests coming from sales or other stakeholders.
  • Go/No-Go Decision: She determines if the Academy team has the capacity to support the event.
  • Project Tracking: Once confirmed, Rachel adds the event to the Monday board with all details and assigns the presenter.

2. Information Gathering & Stakeholder Alignment

  • The Essentials: Collect key items from Rachel, including conference details, registration info, and the list of accepted sessions.
  • "Know Before You Go" (KBYG): Attend the KBYG meeting (typically a calendar invite) to ensure alignment with all stakeholders.
  • Planning Sheet Review: Review the event planning sheet to identify booth locations and specific logistical requirements dictated by the sales manager.
  • Team Synergy: Identify if a co-presenter is assigned to begin coordinating roles early.

3. Travel & Registration

  • Booking Travel: Presenters are responsible for booking their own travel once confirmed.
  • Team Visibility: Add travel logistics into the Monday board item comments to keep everyone informed.
  • Portal Management: Complete your conference registration and manage any "to-do" items in the conference portal.

Phase 2: Session Preparation & Environment Setup

This phase focuses on building your digital staging area and finalizing the slide deck using the lead capture "gate" workflow.

1. The Staging Area

  • Drive Navigation: Go to the Academy Team Shared Drive > Staging Area.
  • Search First: Check to see if a staging folder for the event has already been created before making a new one.
  • Folder Creation: If one does not exist, create a folder named [Event Name] - [Date] to match the Monday board.

2. Slide Deck Foundations & IP Protection

  • Make a copy of the session deck, move the deck into the event folder you just created.
  • Standard (1 hour): Use the source decks in the standard session folder.
  • Extended (1.5-2 hours): Copy a more recent conference session deck that matches the timing.
  • Full-Day Workshops: If the event is a full-day workshop, make a copy of the source workshop deck
  • Edit the slide deck: Edit the slide deck title, bio slides, bitlys, back channel, ending slides, etc.
  • Web Publishing: Go to File > Share > Publish to web to generate a public link.

3. Lead Capture, Booth Awareness, & Link Management

  • Determine Strategy: Choose your lead capture method based the situation: [placeholder text]
  • Determine Strategy: Choose your lead capture method based on session type and length: [placeholder text]
  • SOP Google Form Attendee Collection (The "Gate"): Follow the Attendee Collection SOP to copy the master form and set up the automated redirect.
  • Custom Short Link (bitly): Use Karl Hehr's bitly account (via LastPass) to create an ampd.it bitly

4. Slide Deck prep and editing

  • The "Manual Grind": Methodically replace all old bitly links, QR codes, and back-channel links throughout the deck with your new event-specific versions.
  • Booth & Session Awareness: Edit the wrap-up section of your deck to include a Come see us slide that lists your booth location and any other sessions you are hosting at the conference.

Phase 3: Engagement & Support Setup

This covers the tools used to interact with the audience in real-time.

1. Back-Channel Setup

  • Context for Use: While typically for full-day workshops, back-channels should be used for conferences if there are two or more sessions.
  • The "Homebase": The back-channel acts as a homebase for the entire conference, allowing attendees to see questions from other sessions and track your schedule.
  • Make a copy of the Back Channel document template. Edit for your event, change sharing permissions, link in slide deck and add to Monday comments for the back channel support POC.
  • Support: Request Tammy from the Support Team to assign a team member to monitor the back-channel in the Monday Board. Assign “Tammy Groff” in the Support POC column and tag her in a comment within the Monday board event item. She will assign someone on her team. Make sure to add in session time and timezone information and a link to the back channel. Do this at least 2 weeks prior and it is recommended that you check who is your support person and connect with them the day before.

2. Slido (Optional Lead Capture Alternative)

  • Usage: Slido is an alternative way to add interaction or capture leads, often for longer sessions (1.5-3 hours, or full day).
  • Login Barrier: Note that to capture emails via Slido, you must force participants to login. This can be a barrier that lowers overall engagement compared to the Google Form "gate."

Phase 4: Post-Conference & Wrap-Up

This final phase ensures data is secured and leads are shared with marketing.

1. Data & Communication Management

  • Attendance: Ensure all form leads and attendee data are placed in the staging folder for Rachel.
  • Lead Sharing: Provide the finalized attendee list to academy@amplifiedit.cdw.com (or add to folder).
  • Back-Channel Cleanup: Resolve and answer all outstanding questions in the back-channel before starting outreach. Remove empty rows.
  • Attendee Outreach: Send the follow-up email to attendees one week after the event.

2. Internal Debrief

  • Formal Review: Conduct a post-event debrief in the debrief document.
  • Team Meeting: Provide a short debrief summary in the team meeting document.
Quick Action Checklist

  • Create bitly going to either the Google Form or Web Published URL
  • Replace title and all bitlys in slide deck
  • Booth/Session awareness slide added to the deck.
  • Back-channel created and support assigned (if 2+ sessions).
  • Attendance data saved to staging folder.
  • Leads shared with Rachel.
  • Follow-up email sent with resources and feedback link.
